Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before treatment.

What is laser hair removal?

Laser hair removal is a non-invasive cosmetic procedure that uses concentrated light energy to target pigment in the hair and impair the hair follicle, producing long-term hair reduction.

Is laser hair removal permanent?

It provides long-term hair reduction. Some clients may require occasional maintenance sessions over time.

How many sessions will I need?

Most clients require approximately six to eight sessions, although the number varies according to the treatment area, hair growth, skin type and individual response.

Does laser hair removal hurt?

Most clients describe the sensation as mild warmth or a quick snapping feeling. Comfort varies by person and treatment area.

Can all skin types be treated?

The clinic's technology may be suitable for a broad range of skin tones. A consultation and patch test help determine the safest settings for each client.

What should I do before my appointment?

Shave the treatment area approximately 24 hours before your appointment. Avoid waxing, plucking, threading, tanning and excessive sun exposure.

Can laser hair removal help with ingrown hairs?

Yes. Reducing hair growth can also reduce ingrown hairs, razor bumps and irritation caused by shaving or waxing.

Is there any downtime?

Most clients return to normal activities immediately. Temporary redness or sensitivity may occur after treatment.

Can men receive laser hair removal?

Yes. Common treatment areas for men include the beard line, neck, chest, back, shoulders and abdomen.
